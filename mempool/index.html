Commission

When the gold hoop is empty it rests at the base of the lattice, wide around the circumference. As sends arrive and near inclusion it climbs, contracts, and tilts so the next-in tx sits nearest the tip.

When that hoop reaches the cyan forming block, those sends merge into it. Each cyan seat is one hasher’s valid-hash bonus (pending hash vouts). Immature until 6-conf. Client hash totals never paint a seat.

At blockfound the cyan hoop tilts to the tip and miner rewards firework in — graphics only, no lettering.
