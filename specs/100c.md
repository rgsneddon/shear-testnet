# Spec 100c — FS-1 spend generators

Owner ruling G2 (05:08 BST) amends §1.5 and §1.5.1 to the derivation `shear-fs1/src/generators.rs` already uses. The points are unchanged. The set remains pending PA+CA ratification. It is not a ratified consensus set.

The domain `shear-fs1-generators-v1` is version-bound: the same points on every network.

## 1.5 Spend generators

`G` in the spend proof is only the FCMP commitment blind. Spend auth uses `B`.

### `E(H)` and `B`

Shear's note blinding generator `H` is a Ristretto255 point:

```text
H = from_uniform_bytes(SHA-512("shear-bpplus-v2" || "shear-note-H-v1"))
```

`from_uniform_bytes` is the Ristretto map of that 64-byte SHA-512 digest. `E` maps the compressed encoding of `H` onto Ed25519 (`ristretto_to_ed` in `shear-fs1/src/leaf.rs`). It rejects a representative with an odd `s`, decodes the Edwards point, and clears the cofactor: `E(P) = 8⁻¹·(8·P)`. `E(H)` is the proof's `G`.

`B` is the Ed25519 basepoint. SA+L multiplies the spend secret by `B`.

| Point | Role | Compressed encoding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `G` = `E(H)` | FCMP commitment blind | `132ba4e11d99a33cadb5e223a5ae090ea51062e34b954440f5825289a44a65ec` |
| `B` | spend auth | `5866666666666666666666666666666666666666666666666666666666666666` |

### `T`, `U`, and `V`

`T`, `U`, and `V` are Ed25519 points from Blake2b try-and-increment, the same procedure as `ed25519_point` in `shear-fs1/src/generators.rs`. This is not RFC 9380 and not Elligator.

The domain separator `DST` is the ASCII bytes `shear-fs1-generators-v1`. For `ctr = 0, 1, ...`:

```text
h = Blake2b-512(DST || 0x00 || curve || 0x00 || label || u32le(index) || u32le(ctr))
```

Both `0x00` bytes are single separators. `curve` and `label` are raw ASCII, with no length prefix. `u32le` is four bytes, little-endian. For `T`, `U`, and `V`, `curve` is `ed25519`, `label` is one byte (`0x54`, `0x55`, or `0x56`), and `index` is `0`:

```text
h = Blake2b-512(DST || 0x00 || "ed25519" || 0x00 || label || u32le(0) || u32le(ctr))
```

The first 32 bytes of `h` are a compressed Edwards Y coordinate. The first `ctr` whose encoding decompresses, and whose point multiplied by the cofactor 8 is not the identity, wins. That cofactor-cleared point is the generator.

| Point | Label byte | Index | Compressed encoding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `T` | `0x54` | 0 | `d5f3591add3283f2b17df466d2d38a4786e4befe6f4b387e8fef06fed832f1f8` |
| `U` | `0x55` | 0 | `762337445e090cff30ef10c2c6960e5ba00f89c619f2e6eae9596170b469864d` |
| `V` | `0x56` | 0 | `a4f2194967473f8c323bb301a02a1f48214f779e346662c5adfc41a30fa84f89` |

`U` and `V` use this procedure under their own labels. No other derivation defines `T`, `U`, or `V`.

Selene and Helios points in the same digest use that Blake2b input with `curve` equal to the ASCII `selene` or `helios` and cofactor 1 (the decoded non-identity point is used as-is). Their labels are:

| Curve | Label | Index |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `selene` | `g`, `h`, `init` | 0 |
| `selene` | `g_bold`, `h_bold` | `0 .. 2048` |
| `helios` | `g`, `h`, `init` | 0 |
| `helios` | `g_bold`, `h_bold` | `0 .. 1024` |

The row counts are `Fcmp::ipa_rows(8, 7)`: 2,048 on Selene and 1,024 on Helios.

### Digest

`generators_digest` is Blake2b-512 over `DST` and then every compressed point, in this order:

1. `E(H)`, the proof's `G`
2. `B`, the Ed25519 basepoint
3. `T`, `U`, `V`
4. Selene `g`, `h`, `init`, every `g_bold`, every `h_bold`
5. Helios `g`, `h`, `init`, every `g_bold`, every `h_bold`

The digest is

```text
4aab173c8065e7e5cff88945399b98cbbb51f647297231d09cb069c82f965288eaaf3ecb9ea88ae4e3de1dde2623710fe5e4f4353c529ef613fac9ff8b0699e6
```

That digest covers `E(H)` as `G` and `B`. It is pinned in `shear-fs1/vectors/generators.txt`. Ruling G2 does not change it.

## 1.5.1 T is defined once

`T` is defined once, by §1.5. Nothing else defines `T`.

`T` is the Ed25519 point from the Blake2b try-and-increment above with label byte `0x54` and index 0. Its compressed encoding is `d5f3591add3283f2b17df466d2d38a4786e4befe6f4b387e8fef06fed832f1f8`.

The spend-generator digest is the digest in §1.5. It covers `E(H)` as `G` and `B`, then `T`, `U`, `V`, then Selene, then Helios, and its value is `4aab173c8065e7e5cff88945399b98cbbb51f647297231d09cb069c82f965288eaaf3ecb9ea88ae4e3de1dde2623710fe5e4f4353c529ef613fac9ff8b0699e6`.

Ruling G2 retires the earlier definition `T = from_uniform_bytes(SHA-512(0x0f ‖ "shear/fs1/gen-T"))` (`e04b89ed…`, Ed25519 `05aed163…`). The same retirement applies to `U` and `V` under that SHA-512 construction. Those points are not `T`, `U`, or `V`.
