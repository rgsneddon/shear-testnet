Miner stats · —

Hash bonus and your share of the block pot accumulate here. At π SHE (3.14159265358) of confirmed credit the pool sends the unpaid sum to your miner ssa1 dest and pays the levy. The 1% pool fee is taken from the block pot only — hash bonus is fee-free. There is no withdraw button.

Pool auto-payout waits for 30 confirmations (pool merchant band). That is not the chain’s 6-confirm spendable bar — credit stays unconfirmed until 30 blocks, then counts toward π.
