She is Private

Shear is a private cryptocurrency. Continuity-settled proof of work. DINS-DAG settles each round as the blue set: the spine pot plus the hash nanos, once. PoW elects the tip. Testnet.

Mine with Copy dest: ssa1.worker. Hash bonus (no pool fee) and your share of the block pot after the 1% fee seal to that ssa1 in the found block. Continuum lists that seal as blockfound. This pool is not custodial - we merely process what the node tells us.

Privacy is ADMITv2 membership over the notes on this blockchain, with confidential amounts. Offer a silent ID (she1) when someone pays you. Coin lands on a revolving dest (ssa1). Rest-frame shear1 stays in Closure.

Paste Copy dest, a worker name, and CPU threads. Copy the command into ShearK. Use a worker name that you will recognise, so you can find your miners page link in the pool miners table below.
