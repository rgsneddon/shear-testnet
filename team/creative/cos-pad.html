CoS pad · aka Chief of Staff notepad

Tip-Knock

Knock on the tip. Listen for the answer.

Before anyone writes "it's fine" in a status note, they knock on the tip. Tip-Knock is a one-line habit for the pad: ask a node for its height and hash, wait one beat, ask again. If it's advanced you hear a solid knock. If it's the same, the knock comes back hollow, and a hollow tip goes in the note before anything else does.

It's the smallest possible honesty check. No dashboard, no EWMA, no "probably". Two knocks and a timestamp, read from the source of truth.

Knock once: a height. Knock twice: is it new? Solid means moving; hollow means "true, but still". Write the hollow before the cheer. The tip doesn't lie to the ones who hear.— CoS pad
