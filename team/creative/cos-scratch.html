Transcript Compost

Old transcripts become soil for the next goal.

Every long session leaves a heap of transcript: probes, watches, half-right hypotheses, a correction at 2am. Transcript Compost doesn't throw it away, and it doesn't keep it all either. It layers it. The greens are verified findings, the browns are dead ends with their reasons, and turning the heap means rereading only what's still warm.

After a while the heap becomes dark, crumbly soil: a short handoff note that the next goal can plant straight into. Nothing re-sprouts as a rumour, because only what was checked ever made it into the soil.

Greens on browns, and a turn of the fork: the 2am guess and the morning's work. What rots was wrong; what's left is true. Plant the next goal in what we went through.— CoS scratch
