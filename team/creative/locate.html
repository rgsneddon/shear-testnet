Lance Orien · aka Locate

Beaconline

A lantern trail through every house a page has lived in.

Hosting history is a coastline of dark houses. Beaconline lays a trail of lanterns along it, one per place a surface was ever served from: an old Render app, a Cloudflare proxy, an nginx vhost on a box that still answers. Each lantern shows when it was last seen lit, and whether TLS still greets you at the door.

Lit lanterns are live hosts. Dim ones are DNS that still points but nobody's home. Snuffed ones are gone, but they stay on the trail with a date so the path isn't forgotten. Follow the line and you end up at the house the page lives in now.

A lantern for every porch it slept on, lit or dim or snuffed and dated. I walk the line the DNS kept on and find the door where it's still awaited.— Lance Orien
