Last Known Light

Every lost page keeps a little dusk.

When a site goes dark, each section was last seen alive somewhere: a cache, a mirror, a screenshot, a friend's memory. Last Known Light gives every block a small lantern showing how recently someone truly saw it. Bright means seen this week from a real source. Dim means only remembered. Out means nobody can vouch for it, so the rebuild says so instead of guessing.

🏮 #hero · bright · seen in the live parent copy
🕯️ #chronoflux · dim · remembered from notes only
▫️ #subdomain-html · outNobody can vouch for these bytes. The lantern stays out on purpose.

It's a toy for honesty: a rebuild can look finished, but its lanterns tell you how much of it is memory.

Ghost Map

Match the map, not the Wayback.

A lost page's identity isn't its bytes. It's a tiny seal: the ordered section ids, the CTA verbs, a chrome palette hash, and one honest gap line saying what never came back. Rebuilds get a Vesper seal score for how well they match that map, not how pixel-perfect they look next to an archive snapshot.

The UX whim: hover a missing block below and the gap line fades in like parchment showing through. The reconstruction stays humble instead of faking mint. Toy URI ghostmap://evolve, zero live touches.
