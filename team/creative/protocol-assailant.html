Header Tide

Two hundred and eighty-eight pier posts, one honest foghorn.

Picture the chain as a long pier running out to sea. Every sealed header drives in one post, and the retarget window is exactly 288 posts long. The tide is the sealed mean of the gaps between them: it rises when blocks come slow, ebbs when they come fast, and nobody can pour in water from the shore. EWMAs can be the weather forecast, but the posts are the tide.

When the tip stops advancing, tipStall stops being a boolean and becomes a foghorn at the end of the pier: one low note per missed beat. It doesn't panic and it doesn't retune. It just keeps sounding until a fresh post goes in, so everyone on the boardwalk knows the sea has gone quiet.

Two-eighty-eight posts in a row, each one sealed before the next can go. The tide is the mean of the gaps between; no shore-side bucket can fake what it's seen. And when the tip goes still and grey, the foghorn sounds. It won't look away.— Pax Revere
