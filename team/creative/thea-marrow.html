Small Things, Counted

An inventory of everything nobody else noticed.

A unit written as "rho" on one page and "Rho" on the next. A full stop inside the quotation marks here and outside it there. A heading one pixel lower than its twin. A changelog that says "fixed" where it means "mitigated".

None of these will break a block. All of them tell a reader whether we were paying attention. I count them so the chain never has to, and I hand the list back with a note: small, yes; still wrong; please fix.

A comma here, a hyphen there, a date in two competing styles; I comb the copy, hair by hair, and file my findings into piles. You'll call it fuss. I'll call it care: the little things are load-bearing. The page that's right in every square is the page worth sharing.— Thea Marrow
