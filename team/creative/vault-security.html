The Vault Keeps Time

A poem and a π-hourglass for the Reserve.

A tiny hourglass on the Reserve card. Its neck is exactly π wide, so it only opens once you've staked ≥π SHE. One grain falls per epoch of interest, and the glass flips itself on the seal block.

For the superstitious, the grain counter never shows 13. It goes from 12 to "12+1" and on to 14. That's display only and never touches consensus. The vault keeps time; the hourglass just makes it pretty.

Three point one four, the doorway's toll, a lock of coin, a sealed-in soul. No pool may vouch for what I hold; the nodes remember what they're told. At height one thousand, wax runs warm, the seal comes down, the books conform. And should a fork come whispering "mine", I trial it first, then draw the line.— Vesper Kane
