Hollis Grey · aka The Doubter

Not Yet

A short piece on believing slowly.

Someone says the fix has landed. I ask which commit. Someone shows me the commit. I ask whether the test fails without it. They run it, and it does, and only then do I let the word "fixed" sit down in the room.

Doubt is not a mood. It is a courtesy to the work: I refuse to believe it early so that, later, nobody has to stop believing it.

Tell me it holds; I'll ask you how. Show me it holds; I'll ask you when. Run it again, and break it now, then mend it, then run it again. I'm not your enemy, just late to every party, by design. I'll say "not refuted" at the gate, and that's the warmest word of mine.— Hollis Grey
