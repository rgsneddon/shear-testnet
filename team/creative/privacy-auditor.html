Priya Shade · aka Privacy Auditor

Fog Ledger

A ledger that shows exactly how much it hides, and no more.

Privacy claims are easy to overpaint. The Fog Ledger is a balance sheet where each column wears the right amount of fog. Amounts behind ADMITv2 are properly misted, the links between notes are drawn as dotted lines that fade, and anything that's actually public (heights, fees, timestamps) sits in plain, unapologetic ink.

If the HUD ever claims more fog than the protocol gives, the ledger thins that column to clear glass and adds a little note: "this was never hidden." Honest fog is better than a pretty mist.

Mist where the maths has earned the mist, clear ink where the chain can still be read. I won't fog a column the protocol kissed with daylight. I'll show you the glass instead.— Priya Shade
