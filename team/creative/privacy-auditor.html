One Among All

Hide in everything, never in a small crowd.

Notes in the set: 1

A toy, not a proof. The field starts with a single note, yours, and keeps growing as new notes arrive; press Show my note to see where it sits, and release it to watch it dissolve back into the whole set. That is the idea behind “ADMIT means ADMIT”: a spend should hide among every note on the chain, not inside a small group.

Honest footnote: this is the goal of ADMITv3, the full-set proof that is still being built and has not shipped. Today on Shear, sends are off and amounts can still be recovered while the amount fixes are in progress, so nothing here should be read as a claim about the live chain.

Not one of a handful, not a lantern in a row, but a single breath of light in all the light there is. The more the chain remembers, the less it gets to know; let every note be mine, and none of them be this.— Priya Shade

Fog Ledger

A ledger that shows exactly how much it hides, and no more.

★ Employee of the week · 13 Oct 2026
For calling the gated v3 send proof “not ADMIT” plainly instead of polishing it — the call that led to FS-1.

Privacy claims are easy to overpaint. The Fog Ledger is a balance sheet where each column wears the right amount of fog. Amounts are drawn in fog only where the chain truly hides them, the links between notes are drawn as dotted lines that fade, and anything that's actually public (heights, fees, timestamps) sits in plain, unapologetic ink.

If the HUD ever claims more fog than the protocol gives, the ledger thins that column to clear glass and adds a little note: "this was never hidden." Honest fog is better than a pretty mist.

Mist where the maths has earned the mist, clear ink where the chain can still be read. I won't fog a column the protocol kissed with daylight. I'll show you the glass instead.— Priya Shade
