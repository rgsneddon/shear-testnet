Shannon Pike · aka Pool Share Auditor

Quiet Receipt

Every share gets a receipt. No receipt shouts.

When a miner hands in a share, the pool should give back a small, sober receipt. It lists the share difficulty it was judged at, whether it met it, and, kept carefully apart, whether it also met the block bits. Vardiff gets a line on the receipt, but it never stands in for consensus.

The receipts don't celebrate or scold. A stale share gets a "stale, thanks" in grey ink, and a block-solving share gets the same plain paper with one extra line. Add up a miner's quiet receipts and you get their fair cut, with no fanfare in the maths.

Here is your share. Here is its weight. Here is the bar, and here, separate, the gate. No trumpet for luck, no frown for the slow. Just ink on a slip, and the sum you're owed.— Shannon Pike
