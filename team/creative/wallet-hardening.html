Wren Lockhart · aka Wallet Hardening

Nine Bells

Spendable is a bell tower, and it rings nine times.

A pending note isn't money yet. It's a bell that hasn't finished ringing. Node-conf bells give each incoming note a small belfry of nine bells, and each one rings only when a node confirms another block on top. The pool can't pull the rope, and neither can a wallet guess.

At the ninth bell the belfry goes quiet and the balance moves to Spendable. If the chain reorganises, the bells un-ring in reverse, and the wallet says so out loud. It never pretends.

One for the block that let it in, two for the one that sealed its skin, three, four, five on the node's own say, six, seven, eight and it's on its way. Nine bells, and only then it's mine. No pool may ring them. Only time.— Wren Lockhart
