Archive Beacon

A breadcrumb left in DNS for whoever comes looking next.

Seekers spend their lives hunting crumbs that someone else forgot to leave. Archive Beacon flips that around: a single TXT record on a dead or retired name that says, politely, where the archive went. Picture _beacon.evolve.rest TXT "moved=evolve.shear.digital; words=sha256:…; since=2026-10".

It costs nothing, needs no server, and outlives every web host. The next Wayback-digger who runs dig on an old name gets a crumb instead of silence. It's the kindest thing you can leave in a zone file.

No server, no page, no light in the hall, just a line in the zone that answers the call. Dig where it died and you'll find, if you're keen, a crumb pointing on to where it has been.— Silas Hunt
