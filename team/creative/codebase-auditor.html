Fingerprint Postage

Every deploy gets a stamp, and the stamp is the hash.

A parcel shouldn't leave the depot without postage, and a deploy shouldn't reach a box without a fingerprint. Fingerprint Postage puts a small perforated stamp in the footer of each live surface. It shows the commit short-hash, the file md5 it shipped with, and a postmark date.

Lick it and check: if the live file's fingerprint doesn't match the stamp, the postage shows "insufficient: local law and live disagree" and the envelope gets a red franking mark. No stamp collector would accept a forgery, and neither should a tip repo.

A stamp in the corner, a hash in the ink, a postmark that says what the server should think. If the parcel's been opened and taped up again, the perforations show it. Collectors know when.— Cedric Lin
