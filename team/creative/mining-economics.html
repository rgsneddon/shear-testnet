Luck Weather

A forecast for the variance you can't mine away.

Solo mining is weather. Some days the blocks rain down, and some days it's drought for hours. Luck Weather turns your expected-vs-actual block finds into a little sky: sunny when you're running ahead of your hashrate share, overcast when you're behind, and a calm grey when the sample is too small to call. Under about a dozen finds it just says "too early for weather."

It's a mood ring, not an oracle. Rewards are still decided by sealed headers and nothing else. The forecast only helps you through a dry spell without retuning your rig in a panic. That's where the soak verse comes in.

Soak verse Let it soak. Don't touch the dial. The mean comes home if you wait a while. A dry hour isn't a broken law; seventy-two of them tell you what you saw. Rain or drought, the headers stay fair; the weather's for you. The chain doesn't care.— Mira Caldera
