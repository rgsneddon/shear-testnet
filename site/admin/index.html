  1. On a Mac, open the disk image, drag Shear into Applications, eject, then launch from Applications. Do not keep running it from the image, a zip, or Downloads.
  2. On Windows or Linux, unzip and open the app. On Android, install the APK.
  3. Choose a password of at least eight characters, then type it again. Tap Set password. That creates the wallet. There is nothing to copy onto paper.
  4. Continuum shows your spendable balance and your she1. Copy that she1 when someone needs to pay you. Incoming coin lands on a private ssa1 dest. Never share a shear1 string.
  5. Open Closure and tap Export shewall.bin. Keep that file with the password. Together they restore the same wallet on another machine.

How to mine

Official miner is ShearK-Miner 1.5. CPU only. Log in with your she1.

github.com/rgsneddon/ShearK/releases/tag/1.5

  1. Download the zip for your system (macOS, Windows, or Linux).
  2. Unzip. Run a self-test: ./ShearK-Miner --selftest
  3. Start hashing:
    ./ShearK-Miner --pool pool.shear.digital:1111 --user YOUR_SHE1.worker --threads 8
  4. --user is one string: your she1, a dot, then a worker name for this machine.

Each found block mints 1 SHE (the pot), split by proven work on the public pool. Each accepted hash also mints 0.00000000001 SHE to the miner who produced it. The public pool may take 1% of the pot only. Hash bonuses are not fee'd.

Community governance — The Reserve

The Reserve is a vortice in the wallet (Vortex tab). It is the only programme that may create extra SHE besides mining. Third-party vortices cannot print SHE.

StakeLock over π SHE (3.14…) into your own portal. That entitles one vote per wallet for the current 400-day epoch.
VoteRaise the hash bonus by one unit, lower it by one unit, or leave it. The 1 SHE pot is not on the ballot.
InterestStaked principal accrues at the variable rate observed by The Reserve oracle. Idle SHE (locked in the last 99 days of an epoch) earns none and cannot vote.
EndAfter 400 days, principal returns to Continuum with interest minted only on what was staked before the cutoff.

The first qualifying stake from any user opens the inaugural epoch. There is no operator start gun.
