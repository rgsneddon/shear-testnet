How SHE is created

No premine, no ICO. Shear is a migration from the GNFP project and users who mined there were entitled to a 1:1 claim of coins GNFP to SHEAR. Shear is now created via CPU-only proof-of-work on a Nakamoto consensus using the ShearK algorithm (a variant of RandomX).

Block potExactly 1 SHE, every found block
Hash bonus0.00000000001 SHE for each accepted hash, paid to the hasher who produced it

The pot

A found block creates that 1 SHE. Mine alone and the finder keeps it. On the public pool it is split by who actually hashed during the round; one percent of this pot may go to development. Hash bonuses are paid in full.

Each hash

Your accepted hashes are yours. Finding the block does not let anyone take another person’s bonus. Public pages show eight decimal places, so a single hash looks like dust; the extra unit is still written into the payout.

Shear does not sell SHE. You hash, or you are paid by someone who already holds coin. Testnet balances can vanish; treat them as practice.

Community governance in The Reserve

The Reserve is how holders take part, and the only place extra SHE is created after hashing. You open it from the Vortex tab in the wallet. Nobody starts the clock; the first qualifying stake opens a 400-day period.

Who may take part

Lock just over 3.14 SHE (π) into your own portal as a stake. That is one vote for the current period. Coin left idle in the same vault comes back with you, but it does not vote and it does not earn. Inside the last 99 days, new deposits still lock, then sit idle until the period ends.

What a vote may move

Raise the hash bonus by one unit, lower it by one unit, or leave it. That unit is 0.00000000001 SHE. The 1 SHE pot is not on the ballot. Each portal votes once; you may change your mind while the period is open. Public tallies show the three piles. They do not name who sat in which portal.

When the 400 days are up, staked and idle principal return to Continuum. Interest is minted only on what was staked.

Vortex, and the vortices inside it

Vortex is a tab in the wallet. It is a drawer of small programs that run on Shear. Each of those programs is a vortice.

Already installed

The Reserve is already here. Stake, votes, and interest live in community governance.

The Join waits for mainnet. At genesis a vault is filled once, then pays claims for ninety-nine days. Whatever is still sitting there afterwards is burned. It does not keep creating coin, and there is nothing to claim on today’s testnet.

Adding another

Anyone can publish a vortice. They host it; Shear does not keep a shop window. The author hands you a vort1. key. Under Add new vortice, paste that key. The wallet fetches the program, checks the pin, and installs it on your device. You never type a web address, and you never pick from a list. No key, no program.

What they can do

A third-party vortice can move SHE you already have, keep a vault, or pay rewards it has funded itself. If the hosted file changes, the old key stops working; a new body needs a new key.

What they must not

They cannot create new SHE. They cannot ask for your wallet password, your backup file, or a join1. key. They cannot raise or lower the 1 SHE pot. Keep serving the pinned bytes for as long as you want the key to work.

Start a wallet from scratch

Current pin is 0.5. Use the WALLET button above and pick your system.

  1. On a Mac, open the disk image, drag Shear into Applications, eject the image, then launch from Applications. Do not keep running it from the image, a zip, or Downloads. On Windows or Linux, unzip the archive and open the app. On Android, install the APK.
  2. The first screen asks you to choose a password of at least eight characters, then type it again. Tap Set password. That creates the wallet. There is nothing to copy onto paper.
  3. You will enter that password each time you open the app. If it is lost, the wallet cannot be opened. Where the phone or computer offers a fingerprint or face unlock, that only works on this device.
  4. Continuum shows your spendable balance and your she1. Copy that she1 when someone needs to pay you. Incoming coin lands on a revolving ssa1 that you do not hand out. Never share a shear1 string.
  5. Open Closure and tap Export shewall.bin. Keep that file with the password. Together they restore the same wallet on another machine.
  6. This app does not mine. To hash, run the miner against pool.shear.digital:1111 and log in with your she1.
