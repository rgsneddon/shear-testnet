Google ML Kit (barcode scanning, vision common, common, vision interfaces and
play-services-mlkit-barcode-scanning) is not open source. Google distributes it
under the ML Kit Terms of Service:

  https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

Use of ML Kit is also subject to the Google APIs Terms of Service:

  https://developers.google.com/terms

The wallet's QR scanner (mobile_scanner) links these libraries on Android. The
ML Kit Terms are Google's terms, not a licence Shear grants, and they bind
Shear as the app developer. See docs/licensing-audit.md, decision 3.
